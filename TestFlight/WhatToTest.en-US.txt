2.3.2 makes the change 2.3.1 started safer. Nothing new to see.

2.3.1 closes the code and link you paired with, a few days after both of you update. For a moment around that switch, your partner's app loses access to your side of the pairing and then gets it back. In 2.3.1, two unlucky timings in that moment could make it decide you had unpaired:

- the network dropping out just as it checked
- two checks running at once, where the second undid the first

Either one ended a pairing nobody ended. This build waits and checks again instead.

WHAT TO TRY

1. Update every device, both of you. Phones, iPads, anything signed in to the same Apple Account.

2. Use it normally for a week.

   REPORT THIS ABOVE ALL: either of you lands on the pairing screen, or is told your partner unpaired, when nobody unpaired. Say which device, roughly when, and what build every device on both accounts was running.

3. Pressing the button around the switch may say "Still finishing setup with your partner. Try again in a moment." Press again. More than once in a row is worth reporting.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD

- If the app said another account had joined with your pairing code, Erase All My Data now clears that notice straight away, not on the next launch.
- That notice now also appears when the code was closed but the app could not finish re-inviting your partner on the first try. It used to say nothing in that case.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES

- There is still no way to see whether the switch has happened yet.
- Just after you send, the status can briefly read "All quiet" instead of "Sent". Only the pill is wrong. Already logged.
