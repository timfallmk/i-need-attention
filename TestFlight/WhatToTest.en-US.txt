2.2 is about one thing: several devices belonging to the same person. Sign in on your iPad as well as your phone and both should work, with no second QR scan and no re-pairing. You do not need to re-pair for this build.

One word changes meaning, and it is the destructive one. UNPAIRING IS NOW ACCOUNT-WIDE. It always was, underneath — the storage it deletes belongs to your Apple Account, not to one phone — but the button used to say "Unpair this phone", which was a lie. Unpair on any device and you are unpaired on all of them. The confirmation now says so.

WHAT TO TRY

1. Sign in on a second device. Any device signed in to the same Apple Account: your iPad, an old phone, anything. Install this build and open it. It should come up ALREADY PAIRED, with your partner's name and your history, without asking you to scan anything. If it shows the pairing screen instead, that is the single most important thing you could report.

   Give iCloud Keychain a moment before deciding. The second device needs the pairing key to sync over, and that is not instant. If it shows the pairing screen, wait a minute and reopen the app before reporting it.

2. Send and receive from both. Press the button from the iPad, then from the phone. Your partner should get one alert either way, and it should look the same to them. Then have your partner send you one.

   THIS IS THE PART I MOST WANT REPORTED: does the alert arrive on BOTH your devices, or only one? This is the thing that could not be tested before shipping — it needs two real devices on one account, and it is the assumption everything else rests on.

3. Answer on one, watch the other. With an alert showing on both your devices, answer it on one of them. The banner on the OTHER one should disappear by itself within a few seconds. It should not sit there waiting to be swiped away.

4. Unpair, and watch the other devices. Do this LAST — it ends the pairing for both of you. Unpair from one device. Your other device should return to the pairing screen with a note saying it was unpaired from another device. Your partner's phone should say they were unpaired by you. Nobody should end up on a pairing screen with no explanation.

5. Normal use. Send, acknowledge, snooze, rename yourself. None of it changed, so anything that behaves differently from 2.1 is a bug.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES

- Just after you send, the status can briefly read "All quiet" instead of "Sent". The alert was sent and your partner will get it; only the pill is wrong, and it corrects itself. Already logged.

- The pairing screen may show a blank name field even though the app knows your name. Type it again; nothing is lost. Already logged.

- Settings, Diagnostics may show "Inbox zones: 3 (1 in use, rest abandoned)" or similar. As long as it says 1 in use, that is fine and cosmetic. If it ever says EXPECTED 1, RE-PAIR TO FIX, that one is real and worth reporting.

- Detection of your partner unpairing still happens when you next send or open the app, rather than the instant they leave. Unchanged from 2.1.

- Answering from a snoozed reminder was broken during development and is fixed here, but has only been tested in a simulator. If you snooze an alert and then answer it from the reminder that arrives later, check your partner actually sees the answer.
