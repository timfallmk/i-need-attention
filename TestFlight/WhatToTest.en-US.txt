2.3.1 is mostly about one thing you will not see: the code or link you paired with stops working once the pairing is made.

Until now, the QR code and the invite link you used to pair stayed valid for as long as you were paired. Anyone who had seen either one could use it later. This build closes them. Nothing changes on screen, but underneath, your partner's app briefly loses access to your side of the pairing and immediately gets it back under their own name instead of under the link.

That switch waits until BOTH of you have this build, then waits three more days so any other devices on your accounts have time to update too. So for the first few days, nothing at all should happen.

WHAT TO TRY

1. Update every device, both of you. Phones, iPads, anything signed in to the same Apple Account. A device still on an older build is the one thing that can turn the switch into a real unpairing, and it is why the three-day wait exists.

2. Then just use it normally for a week. Send, answer, snooze, as usual.

   THE ONE THING TO REPORT: if either of you ever lands on the pairing screen, or sees a message saying your partner unpaired, when nobody unpaired. That is the failure this build is built to avoid. Say which device it was, roughly when, and what build every device on both accounts was running.

3. Once, around the switch, pressing the button may say "Still finishing setup with your partner. Try again in a moment." Press again and it should go through. Seeing it more than once in a row is worth reporting.

ALSO IN THIS BUILD

- The pairing screen fills in your name if the app already knows it, instead of asking again. Renaming yourself in Settings now changes it there too.
- VoiceOver reads the name field as "Your name" rather than an unlabelled text field.

KNOWN ROUGH EDGES

- The three-day wait is deliberate. There is no way to see whether the switch has happened yet; if nothing has gone wrong, it either has or is still waiting.
- Just after you send, the status can briefly read "All quiet" instead of "Sent". Only the pill is wrong; your partner gets the alert. Already logged.
