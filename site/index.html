Please Give Me Attention

Two people, one button. Press it, and the person you paired with gets a notification that says you need them. They can tap back to say they're there.

It is deliberately small. No feed, no accounts, no server. You pair with one person by scanning a code in person, and after that the app is a button and a status light.
