A pairing code that closes behind you

The QR code or invite link you paired with used to keep working for as long as you stayed paired. It now stops working once the pairing is made, so nobody who saw it can use it later. This happens by itself, a few days after both of you have updated.

Answers that stay answered

Replying from a notification is more dependable. An old notification can no longer undo an answer you already gave, and your partner no longer gets a second "Got back to you" for something you answered days ago.

Reminders that know when to stop

Snooze an alert, then answer a later one from its notification, and the earlier reminder is cancelled. It used to arrive anyway, looking like a request nobody had made.

Easier to read, hear and follow

The app now responds to Larger Text, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency, and says more to VoiceOver on both iPhone and Apple Watch.

Smaller things

The pairing screen remembers your name instead of asking for it again. If you used the app before version 2.0, the last of its old unencrypted records are now cleaned up even when one of your devices could not save its own copy of that history first. If that happened to you, History says so.
